# Flour n Sugar Documentation

> **A community and discovery platform for local, home, and independent bakers.**

Welcome to the official product, architecture, and engineering documentation for **Flour n Sugar**. This documentation outlines the platform vision, user experience journeys, MVP 1 functional specifications, phased expansion roadmap, business strategy, technical architecture, and production readiness checklists.

---

## What is Flour n Sugar?

**Flour n Sugar** is a dual-sided platform designed to help consumers discover local, home, and artisanal bakers while empowering bakers with a lightweight digital presence to showcase their creations and scale their businesses.

The platform combines two complementary experiences:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                             Flour n Sugar                              │
├──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┤
│   1. Community & Discovery       │   2. Lightweight SaaS Storefront    │
│            Marketplace           │                                     │
├──────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Local discovery & geolocation  │ • Dedicated tenant URL (/store-slug)│
│ • City, neighborhood & area tags │ • Digital storefront mini-website   │
│ • Category & product browsing    │ • Visual product catalog & pricing  │
│ • Baker comparisons & galleries  │ • Custom units & product variants   │
│ • Direct contact options         │ • WhatsApp click-to-chat integration│
└──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The initial goal is **not** to replace existing channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, phone calls, or email. Instead, Flour n Sugar acts as the **discovery and visibility layer** that bridges consumers with local bakers, standardizing catalogs and making it effortless to initiate inquiries and place orders.

## The Initial Product North Star

:::tip Product North Star
**"Every baker should be able to create a beautiful online presence in minutes, and every customer should be able to discover local bakers and contact them in seconds."**
:::

Every feature, architectural decision, and design choice for MVP 1 is ruthlessly prioritized against this North Star.

---

## Core Pillars & Values

1. **Local-First & Community-Driven:** Baking is deeply personal. Rather than feeling like a faceless bulk e-commerce website, Flour n Sugar emphasizes local neighborhood bakers, authentic stories, and community connection.
2. **Effortless for Bakers:** Home and small-scale bakers are artisans, not IT engineers. Profile creation, catalog management, and variant pricing are designed for zero-learning-curve simplicity.
3. **Discovery Before Commerce:** Enable discovery, exploration, and direct connection first. Complex checkout, inventory, and payment rails are introduced progressively as transaction volume demands.
4. **Leverage Existing Rails:** Instead of rebuilding messaging or delivery infrastructure, the platform integrates deeply with WhatsApp, phone, SMS, and email.
5. **Multi-Tenant from Day 1:** Every baker operates an isolated storefront identity (`flournsugar.com/webake`), preparing the foundation for future custom domains and advanced SaaS tooling.
