# Product Catalog & Units Specification

Artisanal bakery products cannot be modeled using a simplistic "one price fits all" e-commerce schema. A cake might be priced by weight, brownies by box counts, sourdough loaves by single pieces, and custom celebration cakes by tiers or servings.

---

## Supported Measurement Units

To accommodate Indian home and independent bakery conventions, Flour n Sugar standardizes the following unit types:

```text
┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┐

├───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ • KG (Kilogram)       │ • PIECE               │ • INCH (Cake tin dia) │
│ • GRAM                │ • BOX                 │ • ML (Puddings/Sauces)│

```

## Pricing Semantics: Price per Unit vs. Fixed Quantity

A critical UX challenge in bakery catalogs is clarifying whether a price refers to a unit rate or a fixed package:

### 1. Fixed Quantity Pricing (Default)
The baker sets a direct price for an exact delivered quantity.
* *Example:* "Belgian Chocolate Brownie Box" -> `₹450 for Box of 6`.
* *Cart/Inquiry Meaning:* The customer receives 1 box containing 6 brownies for ₹450.

### 2. Multi-Variant Options
A single product can have multiple selectable variants with distinct prices:
* *Example:* "Classic New York Cheesecake"
  * Variant A: `500 g` -> `₹650`
  * Variant B: `1 kg` -> `₹1,200`
  * Variant C: `Slice` -> `₹180`

### 3. "Price Starting From" Semantics
For custom or tiered celebration cakes where final decor, figurines, and tier complexity vary:
* *Example:* "Custom Fondant Tier Cake" -> `From ₹1,200 / kg`
* The storefront displays a clear **"From ₹1,200"** indicator with a note that final pricing is quoted upon customization review.

## Dietary & Allergen Declarations

Every product supports standardized, high-visibility dietary badges:

* 🟢 **Eggless (Vegetarian):** Highly critical for the Indian market, where 40–60% of customers specifically seek egg-free bakes.
* 🌿 **Vegan:** 100% plant-based, dairy-free.
* 🌾 **Gluten-Free:** Baked using alternative flours (almond, oat, rice).
* 🚫 **Sugar-Free / Jaggery / Keto:** Sweetened with dates, jaggery, or stevia.
* 🥜 **Contains Nuts:** Allergen disclosure for peanuts, almonds, walnuts.
