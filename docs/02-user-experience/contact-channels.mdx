# Pre-Commerce Communication Channels

During early stages (MVP 1), Flour n Sugar decouples the **discovery layer** from the **checkout and transaction layer**. Rather than enforcing an in-app cart, the platform turns product intent into direct conversations.

---

## Why "Chat-First" Works for Artisanal Baking

Custom and artisanal baking has unique commercial characteristics that make automated standard e-commerce carts challenging:

1. **High Customization Requirements:** A birthday cake is rarely standard—customers need to discuss flavors, egg/eggless preferences, sponge density, frosting types, custom lettering, and reference photos.
2. **Variable Lead Times:** Home bakers do not hold pre-baked cakes in inventory. Production schedules depend on kitchen capacity, ingredient procurement, and existing orders for the date.
3. **Delivery Logistics:** Delivery of fragile multi-tiered cakes or temperature-sensitive chocolates requires coordination (personal pickup vs specialized hyper-local delivery apps like Porter or Dunzo).
4. **Cultural Familiarity:** In India, over 90% of home baking transactions already conclude over WhatsApp. Forcing an unfamiliar in-app checkout creates friction.

## The WhatsApp Deep-Link Pattern

When a customer clicks the **"Inquire via WhatsApp"** button on a specific product card, Flour n Sugar generates a formatted `https://wa.me/` URI containing pre-filled inquiry text.

### Example Generated Message

```text
Hi [Baker Name]! 👋
I found your bakery on Flour n Sugar (flournsugar.com/sweetdelights).

I'm interested in:
🎂 Product: Belgian Chocolate Truffle Cake
⚖️ Option: 1 kg (Eggless)
💰 Listed Price: ₹950

Could you please confirm your availability for an order?
```

### Benefits of Pre-filled Context
* **Zero Typing Barrier:** The customer doesn't have to explain which item or size they were looking at.
* **Instant Clarity for the Baker:** The baker immediately sees the product name, variant, and listed price without asking repetitive questions.
* **Source Attribution:** The baker recognizes that the inquiry originated through their Flour n Sugar storefront.
