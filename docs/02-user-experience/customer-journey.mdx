# The Customer Experience & Journey

The primary customer experience in Flour n Sugar centers around effortless, delightful discovery and rapid connection.

---

### Stage-by-Stage Breakdown

| Stage | What the Customer Does | MVP 1 Implementation | Future Evolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Discover** | Discovers nearby bakers via search, geo-location, or external shared links. | Landing page hero search, browser geo-location prompt, city/locality directories. | Saved favorite locations, personalized recommendations. |
| **2. Explore** | Browses bakery catalogs, photos, flavors, pricing variants, and dietary filters. | Fast, responsive store profile page with grouped categories and image modals. | AI flavor match, dietary allergen warnings, interactive 3D cake viewer. |
| **3. Connect** | Identifies baker availability, service area, and operating hours. | Visible operational badges, locality distance estimation, pickup/delivery notes. | Instant open/closed status, automated live chat availability. |
| **4. Inquire** | Contacts the baker with a specific product, event date, or custom query. | One-click WhatsApp link with pre-filled message, direct phone dialer, or email form. | In-app inquiry builder with date picker, design upload, and structured form. |
| **5. Order** | Finalizes order specifications and delivery date. | Conducted directly between customer and baker over WhatsApp/Call. | Native multi-item cart, date/time slot selection, delivery address entry. |
| **6. Pay** | Makes payment for the bake. | Offline (UPI, cash on delivery, or baker's private payment link). | Integrated payment gateway (UPI, Cards, NetBanking, Escrow). |
| **7. Track** | Monitors baking progress and delivery dispatch. | Updates received via WhatsApp chat from the baker. | Live order tracker (Baking, Packaged, Dispatched, Delivered). |
| **8. Review** | Provides feedback on taste, presentation, and service. | Informal feedback in chat. | Verified customer reviews, rating stars, photo testimonials. |
| **9. Return** | Re-orders for upcoming celebrations and holidays. | Bookmarks storefront link or returns via search. | Customer order history, one-tap reordering, anniversary reminders. |

## Discovery Entry Points

Customers enter the Flour n Sugar experience through three primary funnels:

### 1. Direct Storefront Inbound (Social Referrals)
* **Path:** A baker puts `flournsugar.com/sweetdelights` in their Instagram bio or WhatsApp status.
* **Experience:** The customer lands directly on the baker's dedicated storefront. The UX feels like a branded standalone site, but includes a discreet top breadcrumb back to the city directory.

### 2. Hyper-Local Search (Organic Discovery)
* **Path:** A user visits `flournsugar.com` and taps *"Detect my location"* or types *"Indira Nagar, Lucknow"*.
* **Experience:** The platform shows an interactive list of active bakers nearby, sorted by distance, with cards highlighting specialties, starting price ranges, and dietary badges.

### 3. Category & Programmatic SEO
* **Path:** A user searches on Google for *"Eggless Cheesecake Lucknow"* or *"Home bakers in Gomti Nagar"*.
* **Experience:** The user lands on a curated, high-speed category landing page (`flournsugar.com/locations/lucknow/categories/cheesecakes`), displaying all matching local offerings.
