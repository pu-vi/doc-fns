# The Baker Storefront Experience

The baker journey in Flour n Sugar is intentionally engineered for **extreme simplicity, rapid onboarding, and autonomous store ownership**.

## Onboarding & Store Setup

A major failure mode of traditional e-commerce platforms is onboarding friction: requiring merchant tax IDs, bank account routing, complex shipping matrices, and inventory policies upfront. 

Flour n Sugar uses a lightweight, resumable setup workflow. The first goal is a discoverable, presentable store; advanced commerce settings remain out of scope for MVP 1.

```text
Step 1: Auth & Store Identity
  ├── Email / Google Login
  ├── Store Name (e.g. "Whisk & Frost")
  └── Auto-suggested Unique Slug (/whisk-frost)

Step 2: Location & Service Area
  ├── City, State & Pincode
  ├── Area / Locality and optional map coordinates
  └── Delivery/service radius and home-baker privacy choice

Step 3: Store Profile & Contact Channels
  ├── WhatsApp Number (Primary contact channel)
  ├── Secondary Phone / Calling number
  └── Instagram handle
  └── About, specialties, working days, and lead time

Step 4: Add Products (Optional)
  ├── Upload 1–3 photos
  ├── Product Name & Category
  ├── Unit & Price
  └── Skip and add products later

Step 5: Store Settings
  ├── Pickup/delivery and order-acceptance preferences
  └── Advance-notice guidance

Step 6: Preview & Publish
  ├── Review the public storefront
  └── Publish now or save as a draft and resume later
```

## Storefront Management Capabilities

Once onboarded, the baker accesses an intuitive, mobile-optimized dashboard:

### 1. Store Profile Management
* **Visual Identity:** Upload store avatar/logo and custom hero banner.
* **Store Bio:** Concise description of the bakery's philosophy, chef credentials, and signature specialties.
* **Status Control:** Publish when the storefront is ready; save work as a draft and resume setup later.

### 2. Product Catalog Management
* **Categories:** Group products into custom categories (e.g., *Cakes, Brownies, Artisan Breads, Holiday Hampers*).
* **Rich Product Details:** Title, detailed description, ingredient notes, dietary tags (Eggless, Vegan, Sugar-Free, Nut-Free).
* **Pricing & Units:** Define primary pricing (e.g., `₹850`) and associated units (e.g., `1 kg`, `Box of 6`).
* **Variant Quantities:** Support multiple weight or piece options (e.g., `500g @ ₹450`, `1kg @ ₹850`).
* **Image Gallery:** Upload multiple high-resolution photos per product.
* **Availability Toggle:** Quickly mark an item as `In Stock` or `Sold Out` without deleting it.

### 3. Storefront Customization
* **Themes:** Select from curated color palettes and fonts (e.g., *Pastel Blush, Artisan Kraft, Midnight Truffle, Classic Clean*).
* **Preview Mode:** Real-time preview of how the public storefront looks on mobile and desktop before saving changes.

## What MVP 1 Intentionally Excludes for Bakers

To keep the platform lightweight and protect bakers from cognitive overload, the following are deliberately omitted from the initial release:

* ❌ Complex inventory stock-level deductions
* ❌ Mandatory FSSAI or business registration validation gates at signup
* ❌ Real-time payment gateway onboarding (Razorpay/Stripe KYC)
* ❌ Complex delivery fleet management
* ❌ Invoicing, GST tax accounting, and ledger reconciliations
