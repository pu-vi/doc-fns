# Initial Product North Star

A common trap for marketplace startups is scope bloat—attempting to build sophisticated multi-tier e-commerce before acquiring liquidity on either side of the market.

To avoid this, Flour n Sugar anchors all early product execution around one definitive North Star:

---

## The North Star Metric & Guiding Statement

:::tip The North Star Statement
**"Every baker should be able to create a beautiful online presence in minutes, and every customer should be able to discover local bakers and contact them in seconds."**
:::

---

## Operational Benchmarks for MVP 1

Every proposed feature, design tweak, or technical task is evaluated against four quantitative benchmarks:

| Dimension | North Star Benchmark | How It Is Measured |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Baker Time-to-Publish** | `< 5 Minutes` | Time from initial Google signup to public active storefront with 1 product. |
| **Customer Time-to-Inquiry** | `< 30 Seconds` | Time from landing on homepage to triggering a WhatsApp inquiry. |
| **Mobile Storefront Speed** | `< 2.0s LCP` | Time to interactive on a standard 4G mobile connection. |
| **Zero-Friction Discovery** | `0 Logins Required` | Customers can explore all products, prices, and locations without creating an account. |

---

## What We Say "No" To (For Now)

Maintaining North Star discipline requires saying "no" to enticing features that do not directly drive immediate baker onboarding or consumer discovery:

* 🚫 **No mandatory payment gateway integration:** Prevents weeks of banking paperwork for bakers.
* 🚫 **No custom delivery routing engine:** Bakers and customers already handle delivery via Uber, Porter, or self-pickup.
* 🚫 **No complex inventory count tracking:** Baking is made-to-order, not warehouse stock management.
* 🚫 **No mandatory social login walls:** Do not force customers to sign up before viewing a bakery catalog.

By preserving this razor-sharp focus, Flour n Sugar achieves market velocity, validates customer appetite, and establishes early marketplace density.
