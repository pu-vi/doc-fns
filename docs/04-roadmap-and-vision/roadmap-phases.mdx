# The 5 Product Roadmap Phases

Flour n Sugar follows a disciplined, staged progression. Rather than attempting to build a complex end-to-end commerce suite immediately, each phase builds upon validated adoption from the previous stage.

```text
Phase 1: Discovery & Visibility (MVP)
   ├── Baker Profiles + Storefronts
   ├── Structured Product Catalogs
   ├── Hyper-Local Discovery & Search
   └── WhatsApp & Direct Contact Integration
                │
                ▼
Phase 2: Native Ordering Experience
   ├── Multi-Item Storefront Cart
   ├── Direct In-App Order Requests
   ├── Baker Confirmation & Acceptance Workflow
   └── Automated Order Status Notifications
                │
                ▼
Phase 3: Payments & Commerce Rails
   ├── Integrated Payment Gateway (UPI, Cards, NetBanking)
   ├── Payment Links & Advance Deposit Requests
   ├── Platform Commission Settlement
   └── Automated Invoices & Tax Disclosures
                │
                ▼
Phase 4: Baker Business SaaS
   ├── Order Calendar & Production Schedule
   ├── Customer CRM & Re-order Reminders
   ├── Basic Raw Material & Recipe Costing
   └── Advanced Analytics & Custom Domains
                │
                ▼
Phase 5: B2B Baking Industry Ecosystem
   ├── Bulk Ingredient & Flour Sourcing
   ├── Packaging & Delivery Box Vendors
   ├── Specialized Equipment Marketplace
   └── Baking Masterclasses & Commercial Kitchens
```

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Visibility (Current MVP)

* **Core Goal:** Establish the marketplace foundation and accumulate supply.
* **Success Metric:** 100+ active onboarded bakers with catalogs; 1,000+ customer discovery sessions and WhatsApp inquiries.
* **Deliverables:**
  * Public baker storefronts (`flournsugar.com/:slug`)
  * Localized search and category browsing
  * Self-service baker onboarding and catalog editor
  * Instant WhatsApp click-to-chat inquiry generation

---

## Phase 2 — Native Ordering

* **Core Goal:** Transition from informal chat inquiries to structured in-app order management.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * **Digital Cart:** Customers add items from a single baker storefront into a clean cart.
  * **Customization Prompts:** Select message on cake, delivery date, time slot, and special instructions.
  * **Baker Acceptance Workflow:** Bakers receive a push notification/WhatsApp alert to "Accept", "Request Reschedule", or "Decline" based on kitchen capacity.
  * **Customer Status Updates:** Automated SMS/WhatsApp notifications (*Order Confirmed → Baking Started → Out for Delivery*).

---

## Phase 3 — Payments & Commerce

* **Core Goal:** Monetize through transaction settlement and remove payment friction.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * **Online Checkout:** UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and digital wallets.
  * **Flexible Payment Models:** Full advance payment or 50% deposit + 50% balance on delivery.
  * **Automated Payouts:** Daily/weekly automated settlements to bakers' bank accounts minus platform commission.
  * **GST Invoicing:** Digital tax receipts generated for customer purchases.

---

## Phase 4 — Baker Business SaaS

* **Core Goal:** Turn Flour n Sugar into the indispensable daily operating system for independent bakers.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * **Production Calendar:** Visual calendar showing all pending orders, delivery dates, and required cake sponge bakes.
  * **Customer Relationship Management (CRM):** Customer profiles with birthday/anniversary dates and order history for proactive repeat selling.
  * **Recipe Costing Calculator:** Input flour, butter, chocolate, and packaging costs to calculate true profit margins per cake.
  * **Custom Domain Support:** Ability for premium bakers to connect their own domain (e.g., `www.whiskfrost.com`).

---

## Phase 5 — B2B & Baking Ecosystem

* **Core Goal:** Expand the marketplace from consumer-to-baker (C2B) into a complete industry supply chain (B2B).
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Group buying and discounted wholesale procurement for specialty flours (Callebaut chocolate, King Arthur/organic flours, vanilla pods).
  * Direct connections with custom packaging vendors (branded cake boxes, ribbons, pastry bags).
  * Listing platform for culinary workshops, masterclasses, and commercial shared kitchen rentals.
