## The Network Participants

Beyond bakers and consumers, the mature Flour n Sugar ecosystem engages six crucial industry partners:

### 1. Raw Ingredient & Specialty Suppliers
* Small home bakers often buy 500g butter packs and retail chocolate at high retail markups from local grocery stores.
* Flour n Sugar aggregates demand across hundreds of bakers in a city to unlock wholesale prices for couverture chocolate (Callebaut, Van Houten), French butter, almond flour, dairy creams, and pure vanilla extract.

### 2. Specialized Packaging Vendors
* Packaging defines the unboxing experience for premium cakes.
* The platform links bakers with eco-friendly, food-grade corrugated boxes, cake boards, personalized ribbons, and tamper-proof pastry containers.

### 3. Equipment & Tool Providers
* Stand mixers (KitchenAid), convection ovens, silicone molds, airbrush guns, and temperature probes.
* Suppliers can list refurbished and new baking equipment directly to verified baker members.

### 4. Food Styling & Photography
* Great photography is the #1 driver of bakery conversion.
* A local network of food photographers offering affordable photo packages to elevate storefront imagery.

### 5. Baking Masterclasses & Education
* Established bakers and pastry chefs host digital or in-person workshops, leveraging Flour n Sugar’s audience for ticket sales and student acquisition.

### 6. Shared Commercial Kitchen Spaces
* For home bakers outgrowing their residential apartments or needing FSSAI commercial kitchen compliance to cater large wedding dessert tables.
