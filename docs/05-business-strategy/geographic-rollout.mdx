# Geographic Rollout & Density Strategy

Flour n Sugar is available across India from MVP 1. Marketplace growth operations, however, should be concentrated in a small number of seed cities to build local liquidity.

---

## Why Local Density Matters

In local marketplace businesses, **density beats geography**:

```text
Bad Strategy: High Dispersion (Sparse Liquidity)
• 5 bakers in Delhi
• 3 bakers in Mumbai
• 2 bakers in Bangalore
• 4 bakers spread across several cities
Result: Customers in each city search, find only 2 options far away, bounce, and never return.

Good Strategy: Concentrated Density (High Liquidity)
• 100+ active bakers concentrated in a seed city across its core localities
Result: A customer searches and finds a meaningful choice of verified artisanal bakers nearby. High conversion!
```

---

## The 4-Phase Geographic Roadmap

Flour n Sugar executes a phased geographic expansion:

```text
Phase 1: India-Wide Availability + Seed Cities
   │   • Self-service onboarding and discovery available across India
   │   • Recruit and support supply deeply in 1–3 chosen cities
   │   • Validate supply-demand match and the city activation playbook
   ▼
Phase 2: Expand Density
   │   • Repeat the activation playbook in additional high-potential cities
   │   • Build cross-city brand recognition and operating playbooks
   ▼
Phase 3: Targeted Supply Programs
   │   • Recruit specialist bakers and high-demand categories where marketplace density warrants it
   │   • Target niches such as sourdough, keto, and French patisserie
   ▼
Phase 4: National Density
       • Continue self-service availability while expanding active supply across Tier-2 and Tier-3 urban centres
```

## City Launch Playbook

To launch any new target city successfully, the growth team executes the standard **City Activation Playbook**:

### 1. Pre-Seed Supply (Weeks 1–2)
* Identify and manually curate the top 30 Instagram home bakers in the city.
* Create pre-built draft profiles with public Instagram photos and send a personalized DM: *"We built your free storefront on Flour n Sugar—claim your slug here!"*
* Secure at least 25 published bakeries across core localities.

### 2. Community Engagement & Influencers (Weeks 3–4)
* Partner with local food micro-bloggers and neighborhood WhatsApp community groups.
* Run local giveaway campaigns (*"Find the best brownies in Hazratganj"*).

### 3. Programmatic SEO Indexing (Ongoing)
* Generate city and locality index pages for each activated location.
* Submit sitemaps to Google Search Console to rank for local discovery keywords.
