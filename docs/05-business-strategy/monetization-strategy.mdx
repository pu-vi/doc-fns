# Monetization Strategy & Stages

Flour n Sugar adopts an evolutionary monetization roadmap designed to remove all onboarding friction initially and progressively capture value as transaction volume expands.

---

## The 4-Stage Monetization Model

```text
Stage 1: 100% Free
(Supply Maximization)
  ├── Free Baker Profile
  ├── Free Dedicated Storefront (/slug)
  ├── Full Catalog & Category Creation
  └── Direct Customer Inquiries
            │
            ▼
Stage 2: Premium Visibility
(Discovery Promotion)
  ├── "Featured Baker" Badges
  ├── Top-of-Search Placement in Locality
  ├── Category Spotlight Banners
  └── Social Media Amplification Packages
            │
            ▼
Stage 3: Premium Baker SaaS
(Business Productivity)
  ├── Custom Domain Connection (mybakery.com)
  ├── Advanced Store Themes & Styling
  ├── Customer CRM & Birthday Re-order Reminders
  └── Unlimited High-Res Product Galleries
            │
            ▼
Stage 4: Transaction Commissions
(Commerce & Settlement)
  ├── Native Cart & Online Payments
  ├── Automated Split Settlements
  └── 3% – 8% Platform Commission per Order
```

---

## Detailed Stage Analysis

### Stage 1: The Free Tier (Marketplace Seeding)
* **Price:** ₹0 / free forever for basic tier.
* **Strategic Objective:** Eliminate any hesitation for home bakers to sign up. Build maximum listing density and catalog breadth across the initial target cities.

### Stage 2: Paid Visibility & Sponsored Placements
* **When Activated:** Once monthly active customer searches cross 5,000+ in an anchor city.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Locality Spotlight:** Bakers can pay a modest fee (e.g., ₹499/month) to appear as the top recommended baker for their locality (e.g., "Top Baker in Gomti Nagar").
  * **Category Spotlight:** Featured hero placement under high-traffic categories like "Cheesecakes" or "Theme Cakes".

### Stage 3: Tiered SaaS Subscriptions
* **When Activated:** When power bakers request bespoke software capabilities.
* **Pricing Tier Example:**
  * **Free Tier:** Up to 15 products, standard themes, standard `/storeSlug` URL.
  * **Pro Baker (₹599/mo):** Unlimited products, custom domain mapping, priority SEO indexing, advanced color customizer, and exportable customer CSVs.

### Stage 4: Transaction-Based Take Rate
* **When Activated:** With the deployment of Phase 3 (Native Checkout & Payments).
* **Mechanics:**
  * The platform charges a modest take-rate (typically 4% to 8%) on confirmed, paid orders.
  * Compare this with traditional aggregator platforms that charge 20% to 30% plus extortionate delivery margins. Bakers happily pay a small percentage for guaranteed escrow, automated UPI checkout, and hassle-free order intake.
