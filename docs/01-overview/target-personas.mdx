# Target Personas & Use Cases

Flour n Sugar serves a vibrant two-sided ecosystem. This document defines the primary customer and merchant segments, their motivations, and key workflows.

---

### Profile A: "The Celebration Planner" (Pooja, 28)
* **Goal:** Needs a custom 2-tier themed cake for her child's birthday or an anniversary hamper for friends.
* **Pain Point:** Frustrated by asking 5 different Instagram bakers for pricing, waiting hours for replies, only to discover they don't deliver to her locality.
* **Journey in Flour n Sugar:** Filters by city (Lucknow) + Category (Custom Cakes) -> Browses portfolios and starting prices -> Selects preferred baker -> Clicks WhatsApp button with cake specs attached.

### Profile B: "The Conscious Eater" (Rahul, 34)
* **Goal:** Looking for 100% whole wheat sourdough or jaggery-sweetened, eggless tea cakes.
* **Pain Point:** Supermarket packaged goods are filled with emulsifiers and preservatives; generic delivery apps don't clearly state ingredient provenance.
* **Journey in Flour n Sugar:** Filters by category tags (Eggless, Sourdough, Organic) -> Finds an artisanal baker 3 km away -> Reviews dietary declarations -> Places regular weekend order.

## 2. Baker Segments

### Segment 1: Home Bakers (The Core Supply Anchor)
* **Who they are:** Solo individuals or small family operations baking from home kitchens on a pre-order basis.
* **Scale:** 5 to 30 orders per week.
* **Primary Needs:**
  * Professional digital storefront link for their Instagram bio.
  * No upfront monthly software costs.
  * Easy mobile catalog management.
  * Fast inquiry generation via WhatsApp.
* **Why Flour n Sugar Wins:** Eliminates the messy "DM for price" friction while keeping their familiar WhatsApp order management.

### Segment 2: Local Boutique Bakeries
* **Who they are:** Independent physical pastry shops, neighborhood micro-cafes, and standalone patisseries.
* **Scale:** 20 to 100 orders per day.
* **Primary Needs:**
  * Localized visibility for surrounding neighborhoods.
  * Digital catalog accessible via QR code in-store or on their social media.
  * Showcase of daily fresh specials.
* **Why Flour n Sugar Wins:** Gives them a branded search presence without cannibalizing margins through high commission aggregators.

### Segment 3: Commercial & Professional Studios
* **Who they are:** Established studio kitchens, wedding cake artists, and culinary academy graduates.
* **Scale:** High ticket items (INR 3,000 to INR 25,000+ per order).
* **Primary Needs:**
  * High-end portfolio presentation.
  * Clear lead time disclosures and custom consultation scheduling.
  * Future SaaS features (calendar booking, invoicing, CRM).
* **Why Flour n Sugar Wins:** Serves as a credible industry hub for custom commissions and artisanal prestige.
